## 2. Daily checks (every 10 hours, before starting) (p. 87–93)

Do these on level ground, lift arms down, engine off, key out, armrest raised.

- [ ] **Walk-around:** look for damage, wear, and leaks. Check the safety labels.
- [ ] **Coolant:** level between FULL and LOW on the recovery tank, checked with the engine **cold**. If the level dropped from evaporation, add water only. If it is leaking, add the 50/50 mix.
- [ ] **Fuel:** check the gauge. Fill the tank before parking overnight to limit condensation. Don't run it dry: air gets in and the engine may not restart for a while.
- [ ] **Engine oil:** wait 5 minutes after shutdown, then check that the level is between the two dipstick notches.
  - If the level is **above** the upper mark, fuel from DPF regeneration has diluted the oil. **Change it immediately** (p. 88).
- [ ] **Hydraulic oil:** lift arms down, attachment tilted down. The level should be at the **center of the sight gauge** at 10–30 °C.
- [ ] **Grease all points** (see below).
- [ ] **Radiator and oil cooler:** clear the fins. Use compressed air under 205 kPa (30 psi) and don't bend the fins.
- [ ] **Engine and wiring:** remove any flammable debris from the battery, wiring, muffler and engine. Check for loose or chafed wires.
- [ ] **V-belt:** look for cracks and correct tension.
- [ ] **Water separator:** if the red float has risen to the line, or code **E:9120** shows, drain it (p. 90). Then purge the fuel system.
- [ ] **Evacuator valve** (rubber flap on the air cleaner): squeeze it to clear dust.
- [ ] **DPF muffler:** remove any flammable material around it, with the engine cool. This is a fire risk.
- [ ] **Washer fluid:** keep the tank filled. Half full is about 0.8 L.
- [ ] **Battery indicator:** green is OK, black needs charging, white means replace.
- [ ] **Fuel tank cap:** clean dust off the back of the cap.
- [ ] Check the air cleaner warning indicator, the gauges and lamps, the lights, the seat belt and the ROPS/FOPS.

### Grease points: every 10 hours (daily) (p. 89)

Grease the fittings on **both sides** of the machine.

| Point | Fittings per side |
|-------|-------------------|
| Lift arm linkage | 1 |
| Link 1 | 1 |
| Bucket link pin | 1 |
| Control link | 2 |
| Lift arm cylinder boss | 2 |
| Tilt cylinder boss | 2 |
| Quick-hitch pin | 1 |

- **Grease:** EP2 lithium grease (manual lists Shell Alvania EP2 or Mobilux EP2) (p. 126).
- **Working in water:** grease generously before you start, and again when you finish.

## 3. Every 50 hours (p. 93–96)

- [ ] **Drain water from the fuel tank** through the drain plug under the cover.
- [ ] **Check track tension** (see Section 8).
- [ ] **Clean the air cleaner outer element and the evacuator valve:**
  1. Open the rear door and remove the dust cover.
  2. Remove **only the outer element**. Do **not** remove the inner element; if you do, it must be replaced.
  3. Blow the element out from the **inside to the outside** at under 205 kPa (30 psi). **Do not tap it clean**, because that deforms it.
  4. Wipe the inside of the case with a damp cloth, then a dry one. Don't blow air into the case.
  5. Reinstall, with the dust cover's arrow pointing **up**.
  6. Replace both elements at 250 hours or after the **6th cleaning**, whichever comes first.

## 4. Every 250 hours: next due at 750 (p. 96–100)

Do all of the 50-hour items at the same time.

- [ ] **Change engine oil**
  1. Engine off and oil cooled. Remove the under cover and the drain plug, and drain the oil.
  2. Reinstall the plug and the under cover.
- [ ] **Replace the engine oil filter cartridge** in the same service:
  1. Remove the old filter with a filter wrench.
  2. Oil the new filter's O-ring lightly and tighten it **by hand**.
  3. Fill with oil to the specified level. Capacity is **8.5 L (about 9.0 US qt) including the filter**.
  4. Run the engine about 5 minutes, then stop it. Recheck the level and top up, because the filter absorbs some oil.
- [ ] **Replace both air cleaner elements**, inner and outer. Use genuine Kubota elements.
- [ ] **V-belt:** at 50–60 N (11–13 lbf) of push in the middle, it should deflect **4–5 mm**.
  - To adjust, loosen the three bolts and move the alternator.
  - Replace the belt if it is cracked or worn.
- [ ] **A/C belt (closed cab):** 7–9 mm deflection at 50–60 N.
- [ ] **Radiator hoses and clamps:** tighten loose clamps; replace swollen or cracked hoses. Also due every 6 months.
- [ ] **Fuel lines and intake air line:** check that the clamps are tight and nothing is damaged.
- [ ] **Clean inside the main frame:** tilt the cab and radiator, open the bottom plates, and clear out mud and debris.
- [ ] **Closed cab:** blow out the fresh-air and inner-air filters, at under 205 kPa and against the normal airflow. Check the A/C condenser for debris.

## 5. Every 500 hours (p. 101–103)

Do all of the 50- and 250-hour items at the same time. That is the full list in Section 1. Procedures:

**Water separator filter element**
1. Close the shutoff valve and unscrew the cup.
2. Replace the element and check the O-ring.
3. Refit the cup and open the valve.
4. Keep dirt out of the system, then purge it.

**Fuel filter cartridge**
1. Tilt the radiator and remove the cartridge with a filter wrench.
2. Wet the new gasket with fuel and tighten **by hand**.
3. Purge the fuel system.

**Drive unit gear oil (each side)**
1. Rotate the track until the drain plug is at the bottom, then drain.
2. Refit the plug.
3. Fill through the filler port with **SAE 90 (API GL-4 or GL-5)** until oil runs out of the inspection port. That is about **1.0 L (about 1.1 US qt) per side**.

**Hydraulic return filter**
- This is inside the hydraulic tank. Follow the procedure in Section 6.
- On a normal 500-hour service the hydraulic oil is **not** changed (that is a 1000-hour job). Ask your dealer how they handle the tank oil when they change this filter.

**Breather filter**
1. Tilt the cab up and open the filter cover cap on top of the hydraulic tank.
2. Swap the filter and close the cap.

**Hydraulic oil filter**
1. Tilt the cab up and open the filter cover cap.
2. Remove the filter with a wrench, fit the new one, and close the cap.

**Purge the fuel system** (p. 109)
1. Fill the fuel tank.
2. Turn the key to **RUN**, not START. The system purges itself in about 1 minute.
3. If the engine dies after starting, repeat.

## 6. Every 1000 hours: at 1000 (p. 103–104)

Do all of the 50-, 250- and 500-hour items at the same time.

**Change the hydraulic oil and replace the suction and return filters**
1. Let the oil cool. Park on firm level ground, lower the arms, and stop the engine.
2. Remove the front cover with the drain plug and drain the oil.
3. Remove the tank cover bolts.
4. Replace the **suction filter**.
5. Loosen the set bolt and replace the **return filter**.
6. Refit the cover and tighten the drain plug.
7. Fill through the oil-filling plug on top of the tank pipe.
8. Run the engine and cycle the lift arm and bucket.
9. Lower the arms, stop the engine, and top up to the **center of the gauge**.
10. Capacity: about **20 L (5.3 US gal) in the tank, 48 L (12.7 US gal) for the whole system**.

**Adjust engine valve clearance**: dealer job.

**Closed cab: replace the front window roller**: dealer job.

**If you run a hydraulic breaker or hammer, shorten these intervals** (p. 104):

| Hammer share of work | Hydraulic oil | Return filter | Suction filter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal work | 1000 hrs | 500 hrs (first at 250) | 1000 hrs |
| 20% | 800 hrs | 300 hrs | 1000 hrs |
| 40% | 400 hrs | 300 hrs | 1000 hrs |
| 60% | 300 hrs | 100 hrs | 1000 hrs |
| More than 60% | 200 hrs | 100 hrs | 1000 hrs |

## 8. Tracks (p. 94, 110)

**Checking tension**
1. Clear any rocks out of the track first.
2. Lift that side with the attachment and put **jack stands under the main frame**. Never get under the machine or rely on the hydraulics to hold it.
3. Measure the gap between the **outer end of a track roller** and the **inside surface of the track**. The spec is **20–30 mm (about 0.8–1.2 in.)**.

**Adjusting**
- First remove the cover over the grease fitting (2 bolts).
- **To tighten:** pump grease into the fitting.
- **To loosen:** back the grease fitting out a **few turns, slowly**. Grease escapes under high pressure. Don't remove the fitting, and don't work under the machine.
- When you're done, retighten the fitting and refit the cover.

**Why it matters**
- Too tight wears the track and the drive faster.
- Too loose lets the track hit the sprocket and can make it come off.
- Packed mud makes the track read tight, so clean it out before you measure.

## 9. Fluids and capacities (p. 27, 100–103, 107, 126–127)

| Fluid | Spec | Capacity |
|-------|------|----------|
| **Engine oil** | API **CJ-4**, DPF-compatible. Above 25 °C: SAE 30, 10W-30 or 15W-40. 0–25 °C: SAE 20, 10W-30 or 15W-40. Below 0 °C: SAE 10W or 10W-30. **[not in manual]:** CJ-4 has been discontinued. Current **API CK-4** oils are backward-compatible with CJ-4 and are what you'll find in stores (e.g. 15W-40 CK-4). | **8.5 L / ~9.0 US qt** with filter |
| **Hydraulic oil** | ISO VG 46 (ISO 32 for cold, ISO 68 for hot). Factory fill is **Idemitsu Daphne Super Hydro 46ST**. Equivalents listed: Shell Tellus S2 M46, Mobil DTE 10 Excel 46. **Don't mix different oils.** | Tank ~20 L / 5.3 gal; whole system 48 L / 12.7 gal |
| **Drive unit (final drive) oil** | SAE 90, API GL-4 or GL-5, all seasons | ~1.0 L / 1.1 qt **per side** |
| **Coolant** | 50% antifreeze, 50% fresh water (antifreeze ratio no higher than 50%). Don't mix antifreeze types. | 9.5 L / ~10 US qt |
| **Fuel** | **Ultra-low-sulfur diesel only** (S15). No. 2-D above −5 °C, No. 1-D below −5 °C. Cetane 45 minimum. | 82 L / ~21.7 US gal |
| **Grease** | EP2 (Shell Alvania EP2, Mobilux EP2) | n/a |

Wrong fuel, wrong oil, or skipped fuel filter changes all shorten DPF life (p. 101, 127).
