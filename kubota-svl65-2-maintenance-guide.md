## Daily Pre-Operation Checklist

Perform before every use:

- [ ] Check engine oil level (dipstick — add if below MIN mark)
- [ ] Check hydraulic fluid level (sight glass on tank)
- [ ] Check coolant level (overflow reservoir and radiator)
- [ ] Inspect for fuel, oil, or hydraulic fluid leaks underneath machine
- [ ] Inspect tracks for damage, missing pins, or debris lodged in undercarriage
- [ ] Check track tension (sag measurement — see Track Maintenance section)
- [ ] Check tire/wheel drive sprocket for wear
- [ ] Inspect bucket/attachment pins and hardware
- [ ] Verify all safety devices and seat belt function
- [ ] Check instrument panel warning lights on startup

  - Replace only if contaminated — do not clean
- [ ] Inspect hydraulic return filter — replace if restricted
- [ ] Check drive unit oil level (both sprocket drives)
- [ ] Inspect track pins and bushings for wear

## Greasing Points

Grease all points every **50 hours** using multi-purpose grease (NLGI No. 2).

**Boom/Arm Assembly:**
- Boom pivot pins (left and right sides)
- Boom cylinder rod end and base end pins
- Bucket/attachment tilt cylinder pins
- Bucket hinge pin

**Undercarriage:**
- Front idler wheel pivot (each side)
- Track tensioner grease fitting (track adjustment — see Track Maintenance)
- Rear drive sprocket bearings (if grease fittings present)

**Operator Area:**
- Door hinges
- Attachment mounting plate pivot

> **Tip:** Wipe fittings clean before greasing. Pump until fresh grease purges from the seal. Wipe off excess to prevent dirt buildup.

## Track Maintenance

### Checking Track Tension

1. Park machine on flat ground and lower the boom/bucket to the ground.
2. Measure track sag at the midpoint between the front idler and rear sprocket.
3. **Correct sag:** Approximately 10–20 mm (0.4–0.8 in.) of deflection when pressed by hand.
4. If sag is greater than 20 mm — **tighten**.
5. If sag is less than 10 mm or track feels very tight — **loosen**.

### Adjusting Track Tension

**To tighten:**
- Locate the grease fitting on the track tensioner (front idler assembly).
- Add grease with a grease gun until desired tension is reached.
- Recheck sag after adjustment.

**To loosen:**
- Carefully open the bleed valve on the tensioner (counterclockwise, slowly).
- Allow grease to escape until desired sag is achieved.
- Close bleed valve.

> **Warning:** Never over-tighten tracks. Excessive tension causes accelerated wear to the track, rollers, and final drive.

### Track Inspection Points
- Check for cracks, cuts, or chunking in rubber track
- Inspect track guide lugs for wear or breakage
- Check steel rollers (top and bottom) for seal leaks or flat spots
- Inspect front idler for wear
- Check drive sprocket teeth for wear or damage

## Engine Air Filter Service

### When to Service
- Every 250 hours OR
- When the air filter restriction indicator (if equipped) shows red OR
- Visibly dirty

### Cleaning the Primary Element (3A111-19130)
1. Remove the air filter housing cover.
2. Carefully remove the primary (outer) element.
3. Tap the element gently on a flat surface to dislodge loose dust — do not strike hard.
4. Blow compressed air from the **inside out** at less than 30 psi.
5. Inspect for holes, tears, or damage — replace if any found.
6. **Maximum 5 cleanings** — replace element after 5 cleanings regardless of appearance.
7. Reinstall carefully — ensure the element seats fully and the cover seals properly.

### Secondary Element (5980026110)
- **Never clean** the secondary (inner) element.
- Replace only if it is visibly contaminated.
- Replace any time you replace the primary element due to contamination (not routine cleaning).
